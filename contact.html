FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get started?
Within 48 hours of signing up, our onboarding team will contact you to schedule a farm assessment. Most accounts are fully operational within 1 week.
Do I need any special hardware?
Our Starter plan works entirely via mobile app. Pro and Enterprise plans include IoT sensor kits that we ship and install for you at no additional setup cost.
Is the service available in my language?
Yes! Our app is available in Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Punjabi, Gujarati, and English. Support is available in all 8 languages.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitment required. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your dashboard at any time.
