Who We Are

We Believe Farming is Science & Soul

Stackly Farm was founded with a simple belief: that the earth gives generously to those who listen carefully. We combine deep agronomy expertise with the best modern technology has to offer.

Our team of 240+ farm scientists, AI engineers, and agronomists works alongside 12,000 farmers globally to create solutions that are practical, sustainable, and profitable.

From soil health monitoring to AI-driven market predictions, everything we build is designed to empower farmers — not replace them.
